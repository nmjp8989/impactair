Artwork Specifications

Make every detail count. Start with the right template and crisp, production-ready artwork.

Start with your production template.

Ask our team for the template for your booked billboard. Confirm the print size, safe area, bleed, working scale, colour profile and required image resolution before exporting.

High-quality PDF

Supply a print-ready PDF using the settings provided with your confirmed template. Embed all linked images.

Sharp type & logos

Use vector logos and outline text in the final print file. Keep an editable copy of your original artwork.

Respect the safe area

Keep key messages, logos and contact details inside the approved safe area. Extend backgrounds into the specified bleed.

Send an approval preview

Include a JPG preview with the final PDF so the layout can be checked before production.
