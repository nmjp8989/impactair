Frequently asked
questions

Find answers about artwork, setup and booking.

How much space is required?

It depends on the display size and anchoring. The site should be flat and clear, with room for the display, anchors and installation crew. Share a site plan so we can confirm the required clearance.

Can the inflatable billboards be used indoors?

Yes, when the venue can accommodate the display, entrances, ceiling clearance, power and anchoring plan.

How long does setup and takedown take?

Allow approximately 60 to 120 minutes for setup. The team can confirm the takedown window once the venue and equipment are known.

Are permits required?

Permit and approval requirements vary by venue and municipality. Check with your event organizer and local authority before the event.

What are the power requirements?

Standard 110 V power is indicated for the displays. Confirm the circuit, outlet location and any backup requirements with ImpactAir.

Can I use my own artwork?

Yes. Provide your artwork as a high-resolution PDF, AI or EPS in CMYK. Use the final production template to confirm resolution and bleed.

How do I book for multiple events?

Request a quote with your event dates, locations and brand requirements so we can plan availability, logistics and creative.

What happens in bad weather?

The installation team assesses site and weather conditions before setup and throughout the event. They may pause setup or take down the display when conditions are unsafe.

Contact our team for help planning your display.
