Artwork requirements

  • File typeSupply high-resolution PDF, AI or EPS.
  • ResolutionUse at least 150 dpi at full size for raster artwork.
  • ColourPrepare artwork in CMYK.
  • BleedAllow 3 in (7.6 cm) on all sides, subject to the final template.

Setup & technical details

Power

Standard 110 V power. Confirm circuit and placement with the installation team.

Space needed

Flat, level area with clearance and room for the approved anchoring plan.

Setup time

Approximately 60 to 120 minutes. Allow additional time where venue access requires it.
