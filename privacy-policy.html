Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you browse this website or prepare an enquiry.

Information you choose to share

The contact form lets you enter your name, company, email address, phone number, preferred dates and campaign details. Please include only information needed for your enquiry.

How the enquiry form works

The website prepares an email draft on your device. It does not send your enquiry to a website form service or save it in a website database. You review and send the draft using your own email application.

When you send an email to info@impactair.ca, its contents are shared with IMPACTAIR so the team can respond to your enquiry. Your email provider also handles that message under its own terms and privacy practices.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising cookies or include an analytics or advertising-tracking service. Its navigation and enquiry form run in your browser.

Website hosting

The website’s hosting provider may process routine technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver the pages and operate its service. That processing is governed by the provider’s privacy practices.

Protecting your information

Please do not use the enquiry form to send payment-card information, identification documents or other sensitive personal information. Contact us first if your project requires a different way to share files.

Questions and updates

For questions about information you have emailed to IMPACTAIR, contact info@impactair.ca. This page will be updated if the website’s enquiry or tracking features change.
