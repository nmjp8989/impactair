Artwork Submission Requirements

Trim Area

40′ W × 22′ H
or
20′ W × 20′ H

Safe Area

35.43′ W × 15.74′ H
(40′ × 22′)

17.72′ W × 14.31′ H

Artwork
Format

All artwork must be supplied as a high-resolution, print-ready PDF.

Text

All text must be converted to outlined vectors, ready to scale for large-format printing.

Images

CMYK
300 DPI minimum at 1:20 scale.

Preview

Please provide a low-resolution JPG of your final creative for reference and approval.
