Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting an inflatable billboard.

What is an inflatable billboard?

It’s a portable large-format display with an inflatable frame and a printed face for your campaign artwork.

What sizes are available?

Dimensions vary by package and venue. See the billboard specifications for the illustrated size range, then ask us which configurations are available for your event.

How long can I rent it for?

Book a single event or ask about extended rentals, multi-event bookings and tours.

Do you deliver, set up and take down?

Yes. Share your event schedule and venue access details so our team can coordinate delivery, installation and takedown.

Can it be used indoors and outdoors?

Yes, when the venue has enough clearance, suitable access, power and an approved anchoring plan. Outdoor setup also depends on site and weather conditions.

What are the power requirements?

Plan for a standard 110 V / 15 A outlet. Our installation team can confirm the circuit and outlet location for your setup.

What file format do you need for artwork?

Send a high-resolution PDF, AI or EPS file. Ask for the final production template to confirm print dimensions, colour and bleed.

Is a permit required?

Requirements vary by venue and municipality. Check with your event organizer and local authority before the event.

How far in advance should I book?

Share your dates as soon as you have them so we can confirm availability and allow time for artwork and venue planning.

What happens in bad weather?

The installation team monitors conditions and can pause setup or remove the display if it’s unsafe to operate.

Contact our team for help with your event.
