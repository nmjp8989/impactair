Terms of Use

A few details to help you use our website and plan your campaign.

Using this website

This website introduces IMPACTAIR’s inflatable billboard advertising and provides a way to enquire about campaigns, events and creative requirements. Please use it responsibly and provide accurate contact details when making an enquiry.

Enquiries and bookings

Preparing or sending an enquiry does not reserve a billboard, confirm an event placement or create a booking. Available formats, locations, dates, pricing and delivery details must be confirmed directly with the IMPACTAIR team.

Festival and campaign information

Festival categories and campaign images illustrate possible advertising settings. Event participation, audience information and 2027 campaign availability should be confirmed in your proposal.

Artwork and brand assets

Submit only artwork and brand assets you are authorized to use. Request the production template for your confirmed billboard before preparing final print files. Illustrations and reference layouts on this website are not print-ready artwork.

Email and third-party services

Email links open your email application, which is provided by a separate service. Review the applicable provider’s terms and privacy information when using that service.

Updates and questions

Website information may change as campaign options develop. The written details agreed for your project govern that project. For questions about this website or a proposal, email info@impactair.ca.
